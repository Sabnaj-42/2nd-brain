# GRE Resources -- Master Link List

Official ETS resources are authoritative and listed first (verified Jul 10, 2026). Third-party
resources are reputable and widely used -- re-check pricing/URLs before paying.

---

## 1. Official ETS (the test maker) -- start here

### Free
- **GRE home:** https://www.ets.org/gre/
- **Prepare hub:** https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare.html
- **POWERPREP Online (free practice tests):** https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/powerprep.html
  - Test 1 untimed + Test 2 timed -- the closest thing to the real exam. Sign-up is free.
- **GRE Mini Quiz (10 real questions):** https://www.ets.org/gre/gre-mini-quiz.html
- **Math Review PDF (100+ pages):** https://globalhighered.ets.org/gre-math-review
- **Math Conventions PDF:** https://globalhighered.ets.org/gre-mathematical-conventions
- **Test structure & timing:** https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/test-structure.html
- **Test content (sections):** https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/content.html
  - Verbal: https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/content/verbal-reasoning.html
  - Quant: https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/content/quantitative-reasoning.html
  - Analytical Writing: https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/content/analytical-writing.html

## 2. Reputable third-party (verify before subscribing)

- **GregMat+** -- https://www.gregmat.com (cheapest high-quality course, ~$5/month; strategy videos + schedules)
- **PrepSwift** -- https://www.prepswift.com (free Quant from GregMat; great for quant-heavy prep)
- **Magoosh** -- https://gre.magoosh.com (paid prep; excellent **free** vocab flashcards app + blog)
  - Free vocab app: https://gre.magoosh.com/flashcards
- **Manhattan Prep** -- https://www.manhattanprep.com/gre (free practice test; strong quant)
- **Kaplan** -- https://www.kaptest.com/gre (free practice test + resources)
- **Target Test Prep (TTP)** -- https://gre.targettestprep.com (quant-focused, popular for high quant scores)

## 3. Recommended books

- **The Official Guide to the GRE General Test** (ETS) -- real retired questions; essential.
- **Official GRE Quantitative Reasoning Practice Questions** (ETS)
- **Official GRE Verbal Reasoning Practice Questions** (ETS)
- **Manhattan Prep 5 lb. Book of GRE Practice Problems** -- massive problem bank (great for quant drilling).
- **GregMat's strategies** (online) -- cheaper and often more effective than books for strategy.

> Tip: official ETS questions > any third-party for realism. Use third-party for *volume* drilling
> and *strategy*, then always validate with official POWERPREP mocks.
