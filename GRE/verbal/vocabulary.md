# GRE Vocabulary -- Method + Core Word Groups

Vocabulary unlocks TC (Text Completion) and SE (Sentence Equivalence). The GRE favors
**secondary/nuanced meanings** of moderately advanced words over obscure ones.

---

## Study method

1. **Spaced repetition daily** -- Magoosh free flashcards app or Anki. 50-100 reviews/day.
2. **Learn in groups** of related meaning (synonyms cluster on the test). Grouped learning means each
   new word reinforces several others and helps SE "pair" recognition.
3. **Learn usage, not just definition** -- read the example sentence; note connotation (+/-/neutral)
   and common contexts.
4. **Review missed words more often**; never drop a word until you've recalled it 3x on separate days.
5. **Read** -- dense nonfiction (The Economist, scientific American, Atlantic) exposes you to GRE
   vocab in context, which also sharpens RC.

---

## Core word groups (memorize as clusters)

### Group 1 -- Praise / approval (+)
- **laud, extol, eulogize, venerate, revere, exalt, accolade, encomium** (praise highly)
- **magnanimous** (generous, forgiving) / **benevolent** (kind) / **altruistic** (selfless)
- **astute, perspicacious, sagacious, shrewd** (mentally sharp)

### Group 2 -- Criticism / disapproval (-)
- **censure, denounce, disparage, vilify, berate, castigate, reprehend** (criticize harshly)
- **pedantic, dogmatic, opinionated, obstinate** (rigidly narrow-minded)
- **frivolous, trifling, superficial** (lacking depth/seriousness)
- **obsequious, sycophantic, fawning** (flattering to a fault; servile)

### Group 3 -- Stubbornness / resistance (-)
- **intransigent, obdurate, recalcitrant, obstinate, adamant, uncompromising** (refusing to change)

### Group 4 -- Changeable / uncertain (mixed)
- **mercurial, capricious, volatile, fickle, protean** (changing unpredictably)
- **equivocal, ambiguous, enigmatic, cryptic** (open to multiple interpretations; unclear)
- **ambivalent** (mixed feelings, not "unclear")

### Group 5 -- Scarcity (often the answer for "rare")
- **scarce, sparse, scant, meager, paltry, dearth, paucity** (in short supply / small amount)

### Group 6 -- Abundance (+)
- **plethora, glut, surfeit, profusion, cornucopia, superfluity** (large amount)

### Group 7 -- Boring / dull (-)
- **tedious, monotonous, prosaic, banal, insipid, vapid** (uninteresting / lacking flavor)

### Group 8 -- Careful / thorough (+)
- **meticulous, painstaking, scrupulous, assiduous, diligent, fastidious** (careful, attentive)

### Group 9 -- Words with surprising GRE meanings (high-yield!)
- **candid** = frank/honest (NOT "hidden camera")
- **abstemious** = moderate in consumption
- **affect** (verb) = to influence; **affect** (noun, psych) = emotional expression; **effect** = result
- **qualify** = to limit/modify a claim (NOT just "to be eligible")
- **appropriation** = taking for one's own use (incl. cultural)
- **champion** (verb) = to support/defend vigorously
- **wanting** = lacking/insufficient
- **objective** = factual/unbiased (not just "a goal")
- **approximate** (verb) = to come close to / estimate
- **appreciate** = to increase in value OR to recognize gratefully

### Group 10 -- Useful "tone" adjectives for RC
- **didactic** (intending to instruct/moralize) / **polemical** (aggressively argumentative)
- **detached, dispassionate, objective** (neutral analysis) vs **partisan, biased** (one-sided)
- **ironic, wry, sardonic** (mocking/contradictory tone)
