# GRE General Test -- Format & Structure

Source: ETS official pages (verified Jul 10, 2026). This is the **current shorter GRE** in effect
since **September 22, 2023** (ignore any old ~3h 45m format you find in pre-2023 materials).

## Overall

- **Total time:** ~1 hour 58 minutes
- **Delivery:** Computer-delivered
- **Sections:** 5 total -- Analytical Writing (always first), then Verbal and Quant in any order

## Section breakdown & timing

| Measure | Content | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Analytical Writing (1 section) | "Analyze an Issue" essay task | 1 task | 30 min |
| Verbal Reasoning (2 sections) | Reading Comprehension, Text Completion, Sentence Equivalence | Sec 1: 12 / Sec 2: 15 | Sec 1: 18 min / Sec 2: 23 min |
| Quantitative Reasoning (2 sections) | Quantitative Comparison, Multiple Choice, Numeric Entry, Data Interpretation | Sec 1: 12 / Sec 2: 15 | Sec 1: 21 min / Sec 2: 26 min |

The Verbal and Quant sections can appear in **any order** after the essay.

## Section-level adaptation (important)

The GRE is **section-level adaptive**, not question-level.
- Section 1 of each measure (Verbal, Quant) is of **average difficulty**.
- Section 2 difficulty **depends on how well you did on Section 1** -- do well, Section 2 gets
  harder (but also offers access to higher scores).
- Final score factors in both **number correct** and **difficulty**.

**Implication:** don't panic if Section 2 feels noticeably harder -- that usually means you did well
on Section 1. Never "tank" Section 1 on purpose to get an easy Section 2.

## Test-taker-friendly features

- **Skip & return** within a section (move forward/back freely).
- **Mark** questions to revisit later; **Review** screen lists unanswered/marked items.
- **Change answers** within a section.
- **On-screen calculator** in Quant only (basic 4-function + square root; no graphing).
  Use sparingly -- most Quant questions are faster by reasoning than by calculating.

## Scoring scale

| Measure | Range | Increments |
|---|---|---|
| Verbal Reasoning | 130-170 | 1 point |
| Quantitative Reasoning | 130-170 | 1 point |
| Analytical Writing | 0-6 | 0.5 point |

- The essay is scored by at least one trained reader + an e-rater (automated); the AW score is
  **separate** and reported independently.
- Most grad programs weight **Verbal + Quant combined**; FAMU's bar is **300 combined (V+Q)**.

## ScoreSelect

When sending scores, I can choose:
- **Most recent** test, or
- **All** scores from the last 5 years, or
- **Any specific** test date(s).

So retaking is low-risk -- I only send the best.

## Test-day essentials (quick)

- Valid government-issued photo ID matching registration exactly.
- Confirmation email/appointment details.
- Arrive early for ID verification + security.
- At-home testing has room/equipment rules -- review before booking.
